> ### ⚠️ The 35% problem
> The published rate card claims a **35% open rate**. The real trailing-3-month figure is
> **25.8%**. Every newsletter pitch must use 25.8%.
>
> This is not a presentation choice — it is a delivery risk. Paved and Passionfroot advertisers
> receive post-campaign stats. A brand that was sold on 35% and receives ~26% has a concrete
> underdelivery claim, and any brand that checks before buying will walk and not come back.
>
> **Quote the trailing-3-month number (25.8%).** If someone asks about the range, YTD is
> 28.95% and can be given as context — but 25.8% is the number the campaign will be measured
> against, so it is the number to sell on.

**Nothing in the pending drafts is overstated on engagement** — the open and click rates have

The seven pending drafts have **not** been rewritten for a 63-subscriber difference. The one
exception is the Paved correction, whose entire purpose is accuracy and which feeds a listing
Paved sells against — that has been updated to the fresh figures.

### One number worth knowing for pricing

**1,153 of 2,097 new subscribers came from paid social.** Subscriber acquisition has a real
cash cost, which is a concrete argument against discounting: the inventory being sold is
not free to produce.
