> ### ⚠️ The 35% problem
> The published rate card claims a **35% open rate**. The real trailing-3-month figure is
> **25.4%**. Every newsletter pitch must use 25.4%.
>
> This is not a presentation choice — it is a delivery risk. Paved and Passionfroot advertisers
> receive post-campaign stats. A brand that was sold on 35% and receives 25.4% has a concrete
> underdelivery claim, and any brand that checks before buying will walk and not come back.
>
> **Quote the trailing-3-month number (25.4%).** If someone asks about the range, YTD is
> 28.95% and can be given as context — but 25.4% is the number the campaign will be measured
> against, so it is the number to sell on.
