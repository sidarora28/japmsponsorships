# The five rails — how each one actually works

Every rail runs on email. Gmail is the primary sensor for all of them.

---

## 1. Passionfroot — marketplace, LinkedIn-led

**Primary inbound source.** Brands browse Sid's profile and send proposals; Sid accepts,
counters, or declines in-app.

- **Contact:** Hugo Machado — `hugo@passionfroot.me`. Platform-side rep. He chases Sid when
  brands are waiting, which means *a mail from Hugo is a live deal with a clock on it.*
- **Brands to date:** Reforge, Figma, Replit, Gamma, OMANE, Amplitude, Parlance Labs,
  Brian Balfour
- **Fee:** platform takes a cut. Gross ≠ net. Never discount here.

> **This rail's failure mode is the single biggest leak in the business.** Proposals sit
> unanswered and expire. Promoted ($1,200), Coworker.ai ($480), and Rezonant all came in
> Apr–May 2026 and none were answered.

**Desk behaviour:** any Passionfroot notification → draft a response the same day. An expired
proposal is a total loss on a lead that cost nothing to acquire.

## 2. Paved — marketplace, newsletter only

Brands request a send date. Sid accepts, drops creative into the issue, and **submits stats
after the send**.

- **Contacts:** Yuvna Muthy Nunes — `yuvna@paved.com` (campaign manager); `adops@paved.com`
- **Brands to date:** Udacity (Apr + May 2026), Shift Technologies, HubSpot (×2),
  Superhuman AI, Product Faculty
- **Rate:** $750 newsletter slot

> **⚠️ Payout only releases after stats are submitted.** This has been missed repeatedly —
> Shift's Dec 2025 payout had to be chased into Jan 2026. Money that is already earned sits
> unpaid because a form wasn't filled in.

**Desk behaviour:** the moment a sponsored issue sends, pull stats from Beehiiv and draft the
submission. Put it on the calendar at send time, not "later." This is the cheapest money in
the entire operation — it is already earned.

## 3. 1stCollab — agency-sourced, LinkedIn post + video ⭐

**Best rail of 2026.** Their team sources the brand and comes to Sid; Sid names a rate; they
broker it. Highest value per deal.

- **Contact:** Varun — `varun@1stcollab.com`
- **Brands:**
  - **Airtable** — $1,500, Jan–Feb 2026. Paid out £1,100.72 net on 3 Mar.
  - **Optimizely** — LinkedIn video, live Jun 2026
  - **Recall** — matched Apr 2026, **never actioned**
- **Format:** concept → draft → live post with referral link. Heavier than a marketplace
  placement, and they chase hard on deadlines.

> Sid has gone overdue on concepts and drafts here more than once. This is the rail with the
> highest revenue per deal and the most relationship risk from slipping.

**Desk behaviour:** treat 1stCollab deadlines as hard. When a match comes in, respond same-day
with a rate — Airtable closed in under 24 hours precisely because a number was given without
hedging.

## What this list tells you

Three of the five rails have money stuck in them *right now* for administrative reasons —
an unsubmitted stats form (Paved), an uninvoiced campaign (inBeat), an open deliverable
(CreatorBuzz). None of that requires selling anything. See `pipeline/backlog.md`.
