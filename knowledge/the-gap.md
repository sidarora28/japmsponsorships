# The gap — what $8–10K/month actually requires

## Where things stand

| | |
|---|---|
| Lifetime sponsorship revenue | **$31,600 across 43 deals** (since Feb 2023) |
| Average deal | **$735** |
| Deal cadence | **~1 per month** |
| 2026 revenue to date | **~$5K** (~$625/month) |
| Target | **$8,000–10,000/month** |

**That is a 13–16× jump on current run-rate.** Both price and volume have to move.

## The arithmetic

The target mix:

| Placement | Count | Rate | Monthly |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LinkedIn post | 5 | $1,500 | $7,500 |
| Newsletter slot | 2 | $750 | $1,500 |
| **Total** | **7** | | **$9,000** |

**Roughly one LinkedIn placement per week, plus two newsletter sends.**

Content-load check: ~5 sponsored LinkedIn posts a month against Sid's normal cadence is
meaningful but not saturating — it is roughly one in four posts if he posts ~4×/week. Worth
watching, because an audience that smells a permanent ad feed is the one asset that can't be
rebought. **If sponsored volume goes past ~1 in 4 posts, raise the price instead of the count.**

## Where 7 deals a month come from

Marketplaces produce ~1 deal/month. They will not produce 7. The sources, ranked by close rate:

| Source | Close rate | Monthly deals | Why |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **1. Backlog** | very high | one-time $4–6K | Already offered or already earned. See `pipeline/backlog.md`. |
| **2. Repeat sponsors** | **highest ongoing** | 2–3 | 20+ brands already paid. A happy past sponsor is the warmest lead that exists. |
| **3. Marketplace inbound** | high | 1–2 | Free leads. Only fails by expiring. |
| **4. Cold outbound** | ~2–5% | 2–3 | Volume engine. Lowest rate, does the remaining work. |

### The repeat-sponsor loop is the missing piece

The original playbook jumps from "answer inbound" straight to "cold outbound" and skips the
list of brands that have **already paid Sid money.** Airtable, Optimizely, Amplitude, Udacity,
HubSpot, Miro, Replit, Figma, Gamma, Reforge, Superhuman AI.

A brand that ran a placement and saw results has no vendor risk, no approval hurdle, and often
unspent budget. Re-pitching them is cheaper than any cold list and closes several times better.
Nobody has been asking. **This alone plausibly covers 2–3 of the 7 monthly deals.**

### Cold outbound volume, honestly

At a ~5% reply rate and a ~30% close on replies, 2–3 closes/month needs roughly
**150–200 outbound touches a month** — about 40–50 a week. That is real work, which is why
it's fourth in the order and why the desk drafts them in batches.

## Sequencing — the next six weeks matter more than the rest of the year

Today is **August 2026**. Brand budgets expire in December; Q4 inventory is bought in
**September**.

| Window | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Late Aug** | Clear the backlog. Fix the rate card. Re-pitch every past sponsor for Q4. |
| **September** | Peak outbound. Sell Oct–Dec placements while budgets are live. |
| **Oct–Dec** | Deliver, collect, and use "use-it-or-lose-it" as legitimate urgency. |
| **January** | Budgets reset and go quiet. Do not plan to hit target in Q1 on outbound alone. |

**Being in market in early September rather than October is worth more than any other single
decision here.**

## What "done" looks like

The target is not hit by one big month. It's hit when:

- Nothing inbound ever expires again
- Every past sponsor gets re-pitched on a schedule
- 40–50 outbound touches go out weekly
- Every earned dollar is invoiced and collected on time
- The floor holds at $1,500

The first and last of those are free. Start there.
