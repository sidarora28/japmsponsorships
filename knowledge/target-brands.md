# Outbound target list

Tiered by warmth. **Work top-down** — Tier 1 closes several times better than Tier 3 and takes
the same effort to draft.

The audience is AI PMs and product builders who buy software. Dev tools and AI tools convert;
anything consumer, lifestyle, or non-software does not. If a prospect can't answer "would an
AI PM expense this?", it doesn't belong on the list.

## Tier 3 — cold, good fit

AI/dev-tool companies with a marketing motion, no prior relationship. Bulk of the volume,
lowest hit rate. Prioritise ones that visibly sponsor other newsletters and creators — a
company already buying creator media doesn't need to be sold on the category, only on Sid.

**Qualifying signal:** they sponsor other PM/AI newsletters, run a developer-relations or
community function, or have raised in the last 18 months (fresh budget, needs distribution).

**Disqualifying signal:** no marketing spend, pre-product, consumer-only, or asking for
affiliate/rev-share instead of cash.

---

## How to research a prospect before drafting

1. Do they sponsor other creators or newsletters? (already sold on the channel)
2. Who runs demand gen / growth / partnerships? (find the actual buyer, not `info@`)
3. Recent funding or a launch? (fresh budget, needs eyeballs)
4. Would an AI PM plausibly expense this? (if no, drop it)
5. Q4 specifically: is their fiscal year calendar-aligned? (if yes, December is a deadline
   for *them*, and that is real urgency rather than manufactured urgency)

Log every prospect touched in `pipeline/pipeline.md`, including the ones that say no. A "no,
not this quarter" is a Q1 lead, and the reason this operation has been leaking money is that
nothing was ever written down.
