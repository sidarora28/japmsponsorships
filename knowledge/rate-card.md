## Why $1,500 is not aspirational

| Brand | Asked | Got | What happened |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Airtable** (1stCollab) | $1,500 | **$1,500** | Quoted $1,500 → *"Perfect, sounds good!"* **Same day. Zero negotiation.** |
| **Amplitude** (Passionfroot) | — | **$1,200** | Newsletter + LinkedIn combo |
| **Promoted** (Passionfroot) | — | **$1,200 offered** | Brand-initiated, unprompted. Never actioned. |
| **Coworker.ai** (Passionfroot) | — | **$480 offered** | Lowball. Counter, never accept. |
| **OMANE Media** (Passionfroot) | $650 | **$553 net** | Discounted below card, *then* lost platform fees. |

**The lifetime average of $735 is a discounting habit, not a market rate.** Airtable accepting
$1,500 same-day with no pushback is evidence the ask was, if anything, low. A brand that says
yes instantly was prepared to pay more.

**The OMANE deal is the cautionary one:** opening at $650 didn't win goodwill, it just set a
low anchor and then platform fees took it to $553 net. Discounting on a marketplace costs
twice, because the fee comes off the discounted number.

## ⚠️ The package pricing is broken — Sid needs to decide

Published packages, as they stand:

| Placement | Single | 3-pack published | 3 × single | Package premium |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LinkedIn | $1,500 | $4,800 | $4,500 | **+$300 (+6.7%)** |
| X | $1,000 | $3,200 | $3,000 | **+$200 (+6.7%)** |
| Newsletter | $750 | $2,400 | $2,250 | **+$150 (+6.7%)** |

**Every "package" costs more per post than buying the posts individually.** A buyer who does
the arithmetic — and a media buyer will — sees a bundle that penalises commitment. This is why
steering conversations to the bundle hasn't worked and won't.

**This needs a decision from Sid. Do not act on it unilaterally — repricing the card is not
within the desk's discounting authority.** Three options:

| Option | 3-post LinkedIn | Per post | Read |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **A — leave as is** | $4,800 | $1,600 | Keep the revenue per unit, accept that bundles won't close. |
| **B — flat, no discount** | $4,500 | $1,500 | Honest. "Same rate, guaranteed slots." Easiest to defend. |
| **C — modest discount** ⭐ | **$4,000** | $1,333 | An 11% commitment discount. Standard, expected, and it makes the bundle the obvious buy. |

**Recommendation: C.** One $4,000 package is worth 2.7 single posts at the lifetime average
deal size and takes roughly the same sourcing effort. Locking three months of a brand also
removes three months of sourcing. But this is Sid's call — flagged, not changed.

Until Sid decides, **quote singles at $1,500** and don't lead with the package.

## Rules of engagement

**Open at card. Every time.**

**When a brand counters low:** counter back at card once, with a reason (audience quality,
US/UK share, practitioner positioning). Do not split the difference. If they hold, it goes
to Sid — the desk does not have authority to accept below card.

**When a brand offers above card:** accept the brand's number. Never negotiate against Sid.

**Multi-placement / custom bundles:** always Sid. No exceptions, including "just add the
newsletter for a bit less."

**Marketplace fees:** Passionfroot and Paved both take a cut, so gross ≠ net. Never discount
on a marketplace to "meet in the middle" — the fee applies to the discounted figure and the
net drops twice as fast as it looks.

**Non-cash offers** — affiliate splits, revenue share, free product, "exposure," equity:
these are not sponsorships. Route to Sid without a counter.
