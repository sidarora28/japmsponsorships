# Live pipeline

**Single source of truth. If it isn't here, it doesn't exist — and things that didn't exist
are exactly what has been costing the money.**

Update on every movement. Never delete a row; move it to Closed with an outcome.

---

## Stages

| Stage | Meaning | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| `INBOX` | Inbound arrived, not yet answered | Answered — **within 24h, always** |
| `QUOTED` | Rate given, waiting on brand | Brand responds |
| `NEGOTIATING` | Brand countered | Agreed, or escalated to Sid |
| `WON` | Terms agreed, date set | Placement goes live |
| `DELIVERING` | Sid producing / scheduled | Live |
| `INVOICED` | Live, payment requested | Paid |
| `PAID` | Cash in | — |
| `LOST` | Dead | Log the reason |

⚠️ **`INBOX` is the dangerous stage.** Everything lost in 2026 died here. Nothing sits >24h.

## Conventions

- **IDs:** `D1, D2…` deals · `B1, B2…` backlog · `L1, L2…` lost · `O1, O2…` outbound prospects
- **Value:** quoted amount, gross. Note marketplace fees separately — gross ≠ net.
- **Due:** every active row needs a next action *and* a date. A row without both is a row
  about to become an `L`.
- **Escalations:** mark `→ SID` in Next action for anything needing his decision (any
  discount, custom bundle, date commitment, or non-cash offer).
