## The re-pitch message

Short. The relationship does the work, not the copy.

```
Hi {{name}},

We ran {{placement}} back in {{month}} — {{one specific true detail}}.

I'm booking October–December now and wanted to give you first look before the dates open up.
Same rate as last time: $1,500 for a LinkedIn post, $750 for a newsletter slot.

Audience has grown since — ~75,000 on LinkedIn and 20,170 newsletter subscribers.

Worth a slot this quarter?

Sid
```

**Why "first look before the dates open up" works:** it's scarcity that happens to be true.
There are a finite number of Q4 slots and they're being sold this month.

For the marketplace rails, the message goes **through the rep**, not around them — Varun for
1stCollab, Yuvna for Paved. Going direct around a marketplace that brokered the original deal
breaks the relationship that produces future ones.
