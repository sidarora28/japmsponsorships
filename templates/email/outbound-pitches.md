## 1. Past sponsor re-pitch 🔥 — highest close rate on the list

Use for Airtable, Optimizely, Amplitude, Udacity, HubSpot, Replit, Figma, Reforge, Superhuman AI.
**Check `pipeline/backlog.md` first — don't ask for new money from a brand you owe work to.**

> **Subject:** {{Brand}} + JustAnotherPM — Q4?
>
> Hi {{name}},
>
> We ran {{placement}} back in {{month}} — {{one specific true detail: format, date, what
> went live}}.
>
> I'm booking October–December now and wanted to give you first look before I open the dates
> up. Same rate as last time: $1,500 for a LinkedIn post.
>
> The audience has grown since — ~75,000 on LinkedIn and 20,170 newsletter subscribers.
>
> Worth a slot this quarter?
>
> Sid

**Why this works:** no vendor risk, no new approval, and if their budget is calendar-year it
expires in December. "First look before I open the dates up" is scarcity that happens to be
true — there are only so many slots.

## Rules

- Short. Fifteen seconds to a decision.
- **Rate in the first email, always.** Hedging costs deals; a plain number closed Airtable in
  a day.
- One clear ask per email.
- Every pitch needs one specific, true line about *that brand*. A template that reads like a
  template gets deleted.
- Propose dates, **never confirm them** — that's Sid's.
- Never invent a metric, a case study, or a result.
- Log every prospect as `O#` in `pipeline/pipeline.md`, including disqualifications.
