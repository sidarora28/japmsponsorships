# Collections templates

Money already earned. Be polite, specific, and factual — **never apologise for asking to be
paid.** All drafts; Sid sends.

## 1. Paved stats submission — do this the day an issue sends

> **To:** yuvna@paved.com, adops@paved.com
> **Subject:** Stats — {{Brand}} placement, {{issue date}}
>
> Hi Yuvna,
>
> Stats for the {{Brand}} placement in the {{date}} issue:
>
> - Sent: {{n}}
> - Opens: {{n}} ({{x}}%)
> - Clicks: {{n}} ({{x}}%)
>
> Let me know if you need anything else to release the payout.
>
> Sid

**Payout does not release until this is submitted.** Real Beehiiv figures only — never
estimate a stat for an advertiser. Draft this at send time, not "later": that gap is what
pushed Shift's Dec 2025 payout into Jan 2026.
